Settings
Auto

How far ZigZag looks around each pixel to judge the local lighting. Auto scales it with the photo's resolution, which suits most pages. Lower it for fine print, raise it for large text or broad shadows.

90 %

How bright a pixel must be, next to its neighbours, to be treated as background. 90 % suits nearly every page; 80–90 % is the useful range, and 100 % is too strict.

0

Nudges the automatic threshold. Positive keeps more ink — bolder, but noisier; negative cleans harder at the risk of thinning strokes.

Off

Removes tiny isolated dots — the grain of a dark or noisy photo. Off by default: on small print it also takes the dots of i and j and fine punctuation.
